# Card

Warm brand surface — white on paper with a soft shadow (`elevated`) or a subtle border (`outline`). Never both, never a colored left-border accent, never glass.

```jsx
<Card>
  <h3>Window snapping</h3>
  <p>Ctrl + Win + arrows to snap. DPI-aware.</p>
</Card>
<Card variant="ink">…dark call-out…</Card>
```

- **variant**: `elevated` · `outline` · `sunken` · `ink` (dark).
- **interactive**: adds a gentle lift on hover for clickable cards.
